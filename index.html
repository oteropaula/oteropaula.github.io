Research · Sustainable Computing · ICT4S 2026

E2E-StreamCarbon: Measuring the Carbon Footprint of Video Streaming

Published · ICT4S 2026 congress - Bern, Switzerland

End-to-end framework for measuring the energy consumption and carbon footprint of video streaming. Covers server-side transcoding, network transmission and client-side playback across four codecs (H.264, H.265, VP9, AV1) and three resolutions. Includes a browser extension that visualises real-time CO₂ emissions during YouTube playback.
